## GLM prefill vs Flash-Next (upstream issue #130, 2026-10-17)

- Flash-Next is not faster by technique: GLM-5.3-Flash computes ~8.7B active expert weights a token (8 + 1 of 288,

  64 heads x 512 against 24 x 256. GLM 720 tok/s at 27K vs Flash-Next 1,807 at 6K: GLM does more work per second.
